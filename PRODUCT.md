# PRODUCT

Assumptions inferred from the brief (no design interview was possible while building). Shrey should confirm them.

- **What:** a demo tool that shows inferred reporting lines for a company from a flat list of people and titles, with calibrated confidence and honest abstention.
- **Who:** Firmable's hiring panel (engineers and a founder) reviewing a take-home on a laptop, and perhaps on a phone from a link in the email.
- **Success:** within a minute they pick a company, see a readable chart, understand why an edge exists (or doesn't), move the threshold, and trust the numbers on the metrics page.
- **Mode:** Operate. Scanability and clear data matter more than decoration.
- **Tone:** calm, precise, quietly confident. Like a well-made survey map: ink on paper, one accent, data in tabular numerals.
- **Constraints:** no build step (static HTML, CDN libraries), one Render free web service, cold starts of up to about a minute, low memory.
